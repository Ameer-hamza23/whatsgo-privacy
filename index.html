WhatsGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Overview

WhatsGo is a utility that helps you open WhatsApp chats without saving a number, create message templates, favorites, and reminders, and optionally save WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business statuses to your device. WhatsGo is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

Information we process

WhatsGo does not upload your WhatsApp messages or your device contact list. If you choose to sign in, we process your account information supplied by Firebase Authentication, such as your email address, display name, and sign-in provider.

For signed-in users, selected app preferences, custom message templates, and saved favorites (which may include names and phone numbers) can be stored in Firebase Firestore so they can be restored on another device. Reminders, recent numbers, local chat history, and any WhatsApp statuses you choose to save remain on the device where they are created.

WhatsApp Status saver

WhatsGo may let you save WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business status media (such as photos, videos, or other status content you choose to keep) to your device. This feature works only when you use it. Status media is stored locally on your phone and is not uploaded to WhatsGo servers. We do not access your WhatsApp chats or private messages through this feature. WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business remain separate apps; their terms and privacy policies apply to content inside those apps.

Permissions

Notifications are used only for reminders you create. Contacts access is requested only when you choose to import a contact; imported contact information stays on your device unless you explicitly save it as a synced favorite. Microphone and speech recognition are used only when you tap the mic for voice dictation or voice commands. Storage or media access may be requested so you can save WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business statuses to your device gallery or files. Exact alarms may be needed on Android for reliable reminder timing. You can review these permissions in the app Settings screen. WhatsGo does not request SMS or location access.

AI features

Optional AI message rewriting uses an OpenRouter API key that you provide. The key is stored only in secure on-device storage. Message text you choose to rewrite is sent to OpenRouter and the model provider you select. WhatsGo does not embed or share a shared API key.

Advertising

WhatsGo may show ads through Google AdMob, including shortly after you open the app. AdMob may process device and advertising information under Google's policies to provide and measure advertising. WhatsGo requests non-personalized ads. You can remove ads through the optional Google Play subscription where available.

Purchases

Remove-ads purchases are processed by Google Play. WhatsGo does not collect or store your card or other payment details. Purchase status is stored on your device and can be restored through Google Play using the same Google account.

How information is used and shared

We use information only to provide account access, sync the app data described above, show advertising, optional AI rewriting (via your OpenRouter key), and improve app reliability. Information is not sold. Data is shared only with service providers needed to operate WhatsGo: Google Firebase for authentication and data storage, Google AdMob for advertising, Google Play for purchases, and OpenRouter (plus the model provider you choose) when you use AI features. Opening a chat or using Status saver relates to WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, whose privacy policies apply to those apps and any content you view or save from them.

Retention, security, and deletion

Data is transmitted using HTTPS. Uninstalling WhatsGo removes the app's local data but does not remove data previously synced to your signed-in account. To request deletion of account-linked synced data, contact us at zenith.stack.studio@gmail.com.
